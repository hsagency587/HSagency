Tenuta Montezeglio

Invorio, between the two lakes

Between Lake Maggiore and Lake Orta, amid the greenery

The estate sits exactly halfway between the two lakes, in a position that lets you breathe in the countryside without straying too far from the water.

The open lawns and wooden fence become a natural backdrop for couple portraits, while the vintage minibus adds an extra touch of fun.

A wedding at the estate halfway between the two lakes

Tenuta Montezeglio occupies a nineteenth-century residence set in eight hectares of parkland in Invorio, just minutes from both Lake Orta and Lake Maggiore. The property combines a restaurant, eight rooms decorated in Provençal style and a small lounge for relaxing, keeping the pace of a country estate rather than a hotel.

Open in Google Maps

The civil wedding ceremony is held right on the estate, while the reception can draw on two indoor halls seating seventy each or the outdoor terrace overlooking the park, for up to two hundred guests. The number of weddings taken on each year is deliberately limited, to guarantee care in every detail.

An estate this large offers many different views over the course of a single day: from the park to the indoor light of the halls, my job is to move with you, catching every change of atmosphere without breaking its flow.
